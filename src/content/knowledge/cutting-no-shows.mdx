A missed appointment affects the patient who needed care, the person waiting for
a cancellation and the practice team. Clear reminders and an organised waitlist
can reduce the impact without making patients feel chased.

## Make confirmation easy

A reminder that asks for a reply only works if replying is one tap. "Reply YES to
confirm" is easier than "call us back" for most people. It also gives reception
more time to follow up when an appointment remains unconfirmed.

## Have a waitlist ready to fill the gap

When someone cancels, an automatic offer to the next suitable person on the
waitlist can fill the appointment quickly and save reception from making several
phone calls.

## Mind the timing

Reminders sent too early are easily forgotten; too late and there is little time
to offer the appointment to someone else. A reminder the day before works well
for many clinics, with timing adjusted for the service and patient group.

Small changes to confirmation and waitlist handling can protect valuable
appointment time while keeping communication respectful.
