A no-show isn't just an empty chair — it's a slot a waiting patient could have
taken, and revenue that doesn't come back. The good news: most of the fix is
quiet, automatic, and doesn't make patients feel chased.

## Make confirming effortless

A reminder that asks for a reply only works if replying is one tap. "Reply YES to
confirm" beats "call us back" every time, because it meets people where they
already are. The confirmation also tells you, hours ahead, which slots are wobbly.

## Have a waitlist ready to fill the gap

When someone cancels, the seat shouldn't go cold. An automatic offer to the next
suitable person on the waitlist turns a cancellation into a filled appointment —
often before reception has even noticed.

## Mind the timing

Reminders sent too early are forgotten; too late and there's no time to rebook.
A day-before nudge, with same-day confirmation for higher-risk appointments, lands
in the sweet spot for most clinics.

None of this is glamorous. It's just the difference between a diary that leaks and
one that holds — recovered without a single awkward phone call.
