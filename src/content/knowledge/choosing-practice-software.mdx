Choosing the system your clinic runs on is an important long-term decision. These
are the practical questions worth asking before you sign.

## Where does your data live?

For Australian clinics handling patient health information, the location of your
data matters. Ask exactly which country stores your clinic records, patient
records, files and backups.

## How many systems does claiming touch?

Every extra system between finishing an appointment and getting paid creates
another chance for a claim to be delayed or missed. Ask to see the complete
process, not just the claiming screen.
