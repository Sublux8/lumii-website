Choosing the system your clinic runs on is one of the highest-leverage decisions
a practice owner makes. This article walks through the questions that matter —
and the ones vendors hope you won't ask.

## Where does your data live?

For Australian clinics handling patient health information, data residency isn't
a nice-to-have. Ask exactly which country your records — and your patients' —
are stored in.

## How many systems does claiming touch?

Every extra system between finishing an appointment and getting paid is a place
for revenue to leak. The fewer screens, the better.
