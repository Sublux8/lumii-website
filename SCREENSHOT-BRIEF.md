# Lumii website screenshot brief

The website is ready to accept these captures in `public/product/`. PNG or WebP is ideal; send the clean, uncropped product capture and the website framing will do the rest.

## Lumii for clinics

### C01 — Clinic day

- Status: supplied and integrated
- Suggested file: `lumii-clinic-day.webp`
- Format: desktop, 16:10; ideally 2880×1800 or 2560×1600
- Show: a full day across at least three practitioners, with different appointment states and enough activity to feel real
- Used on: homepage ecosystem and Platform gallery

### C02 — Claims queue

- Status: supplied and integrated
- Suggested file: `lumii-claims.webp`
- Format: desktop, 16:10
- Show: a populated claiming queue with several funders, statuses and believable demo amounts
- Used on: Platform gallery

### C03 — Funded care plan

- Suggested file: `lumii-care-plan.webp`
- Format: desktop or tablet, 4:3
- Show: sessions used, referral validity, funder and next review
- Used on: Platform gallery

### C04 — Connected patient record

- Suggested file: `lumii-patient-record.webp`
- Format: desktop, 16:10
- Show: a patient timeline with a shared form, rehabilitation assignment and recent correspondence
- Used on: Platform connected-care story and clinician page

## myLumii for people

### M01 — Today and Vitality

- Suggested file: `mylumii-today.webp`
- Format: phone portrait; ideally 1290×2796 or a clean simulator export
- Show: Vitality, next appointment, daily health glance and a care update
- Used on: homepage ecosystem and myLumii hero

### M02 — Care hub

- Suggested file: `mylumii-care.webp`
- Format: phone portrait
- Show: the Care area with appointments, records, messages and forms visible or clearly represented
- Used on: homepage, Platform and myLumii

### M03 — Assigned form or rehabilitation

- Suggested file: `mylumii-assigned-care.webp`
- Format: phone portrait
- Show: one useful assigned form or rehabilitation detail, visibly connected to a fictional clinic
- Used on: myLumii care story

### M04 — Health overview

- Suggested file: `mylumii-health.webp`
- Format: phone portrait
- Show: four populated health metrics with readable trend sparklines
- Used on: myLumii health story

### M05 — Personal insight

- Suggested file: `mylumii-insight.webp`
- Format: phone portrait
- Show: a strong but non-sensitive insight card, with its source labels and data window visible
- Used on: myLumii hero and health story

### M06 — Nourish or Exercise

- Suggested file: `mylumii-nourish.webp` or `mylumii-exercise.webp`
- Format: phone portrait
- Show: whichever screen feels most distinctively Lumii—nutrition quality, training readiness or recovery is ideal
- Used on: a future myLumii lifestyle feature panel

## Capture checklist

- Use fictional demo people and clinics only; no real patient or staff information.
- Prefer light mode for the first set so the product feels consistent across the website.
- Keep one phone model, status bar style and capture scale throughout the myLumii set.
- Remove notifications, cursors, debug banners, simulator chrome and development overlays.
- Capture the full application surface with comfortable safe edges; do not add marketing frames or shadows.
- Avoid empty states unless the empty state itself is the feature being shown.
- Keep dates and appointment times internally consistent across related clinic and patient captures.
